## 6. Storia

✅ From the old site: a typical farmhouse on the hills between **Pontida and Palazzago**. The oldest core dates to the **late 17th century**. Farmers lived there permanently from **1760**, growing vines on terraces in full sun from dawn to dusk. The **last extension is from 1859**. Recently restored. Wine used to be made (Merlot + Cabernet red, Moscato Giallo white), now **no longer**.
